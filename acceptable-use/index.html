Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

These rules apply to everyone who uses MiaoCut (miaocut.app), including the free tools, batch processing, the developer API, and any account. They cover images you upload, prompts you submit, and results you download or generate. This policy is part of our Terms of Use.

What you must not generate or upload

Do not generate or upload any of the following. Only use images you own or have the right to use.

  • NSFW, explicit, or sexually suggestive content.
  • Content involving minors, including any sexual, nude, suggestive, or exploitative depiction of a person under 18. A photo you have the right to edit, such as a passport photo of your own child, is allowed.
  • Non-consensual imagery of real people, including intimate images created or shared without the person's consent, and deepfakes or face-swaps.
  • Violence or gore, or content that encourages violent harm.
  • Hate content that attacks people for who they are.
  • Illegal content, or use of the service to commit a crime.
  • Content that infringes someone else's intellectual property or other rights, including copyright, trademarks, and publicity rights.
  • Deceptive or fraudulent use, including impersonation, fake documents, and scams.

Do not disrupt the service

Do not attempt to overload, scrape, or circumvent the fair-use rate limits, or otherwise disrupt the service. Do not create extra accounts to obtain free credits.

Enforcement

MiaoCut does not currently screen every prompt or image automatically. You must not submit prohibited content. If we learn of a violation, we may refuse the request, remove the result, and suspend the account. Where the law requires it, we may report illegal content.

Report a violation

If you believe someone is using MiaoCut in violation of this policy, email support@miaocut.app.

Related policies

Payments, credits and refunds are covered in the Terms of Use. How we handle images and account data is covered in the Privacy Policy.
