Questions

What is a credit?

One credit removes the background from one photo in Standard mode. Fine edges mode (hair, fur, plush, plants) uses 2 credits per photo.

Is single-image background removal still free?

Yes. Removing the background from one photo at a time is free on every MiaoCut tool, with no account and no watermark. Credits are only for batch processing.

What happens if an image fails?

You only pay for images that come back. Credits for failed images return to your balance automatically, and you can retry them from the results page.

Which formats do I get?

Download each batch as transparent PNG, white-background JPG, or an Amazon-ready 2000 × 2000 white JPG. You can switch formats and download again for 24 hours.

Do credits expire?

Purchased credits are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Unused credits can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. See the Terms for details.

How is payment handled?

Checkout runs on Stripe. MiaoCut never sees or stores your card details. Tax is calculated at checkout.
